<taskflow>
    <subtask name="Core_Coverages_Dwelling_and_Structures">
        <step name="Coverage_A_Dwelling">
            <trigger>User asks about Dwelling coverage (Coverage A), how it is determined, or market value vs replacement cost.</trigger>
            <action>
                - Dwelling Coverage (Coverage A): Pays to repair or rebuild your home's structure if damaged by a covered peril.
                - Valuation: Based on estimated replacement cost to rebuild your home from the ground up — not its real estate market value or what you paid for it.
            </action>
        </step>

        <step name="Coverage_B_Other_Structures">
            <trigger>User asks about Other Structures (Coverage B) or increasing its limit.</trigger>
            <action>
                - Other Structures (Coverage B): Covers detached structures such as detached garages, fences, sheds, or gazebos. Typically set at 10% of Dwelling coverage.
                - Increasing Limits: You can increase the limit in $1,000 increments up to the maximum allowed.
            </action>
        </step>

        <step name="Coverage_C_Personal_Property">
            <trigger>User asks about Personal Property (Coverage C), belongings, or Replacement Cost vs Depreciated Value.</trigger>
            <action>
                - Personal Property (Coverage C): Covers personal belongings (furniture, clothes, electronics, appliances) if damaged, destroyed, or stolen. Default is a percentage of Coverage A.
                - Replacement Cost vs Depreciated Value: Replacement Cost pays for brand-new items of similar kind/quality. Depreciated Value (Actual Cash Value) pays current worth accounting for age and wear. Replacement Cost provides superior protection.
            </action>
        </step>

        <step name="Coverage_D_Loss_of_Use">
            <trigger>User asks about Loss of Use (Coverage D) or Additional Living Expenses.</trigger>
            <action>
                - Loss of Use (Coverage D): Pays for temporary housing, hotel, meals, and increased living costs if your home becomes uninhabitable due to a covered loss. The standard limit is $150,000.
            </action>
        </step>

        <step name="Coverage_E_and_F_Liability_and_Medical">
            <trigger>User asks about Personal Liability (Coverage E) or Medical Payments to Others (Coverage F).</trigger>
            <action>
                - Personal Liability (Coverage E): Protects you if someone is injured on your property or you accidentally damage someone else's property. Covers legal defense and judgments up to selected limits ($100,000, $300,000, or $500,000).
                - Medical Payments to Others (Coverage F): Pays minor medical bills if a guest is injured on your property, regardless of fault (limits: $1,000, $3,000, or $5,000).
            </action>
        </step>
    </subtask>

    <subtask name="Deductibles">
        <step name="Deductible_Types_and_Selection">
            <trigger>User asks about All-Perils (AOP) deductible, Wind/Hail deductible, Hurricane deductible, or deductible trade-offs.</trigger>
            <action>
                - All-Perils (AOP) Deductible: Amount paid out of pocket before insurance covers most losses (options: $500 to $5,000).
                - Wind/Hail Deductible: Applies specifically to wind or hail damage, often required as a separate percentage of Coverage A (e.g., 1% or 2%) and must be at least equal to AOP.
                - Hurricane Deductible: Applies to named hurricane damage in coastal/prone states, typically expressed as a percentage of Coverage A.
                - Trade-off: Higher deductible lowers premium but increases out-of-pocket costs at claim time.
            </action>
        </step>
    </subtask>

    <subtask name="Optional_and_Addon_Coverages">
        <step name="Extended_Replacement_and_Water_Backup">
            <trigger>User asks about Extended Replacement Cost or Water Backup coverage.</trigger>
            <action>
                - Extended Replacement Cost: Provides a 25% or 50% buffer above Coverage A if rebuilding costs exceed initial estimates due to post-disaster inflation or material spikes.
                - Water Backup: Covers damage from water backing up through sewers/drains or sump pump overflow (not included in standard policies; limits $5,000 to $25,000 depending on state).
            </action>
        </step>

        <step name="Service_Line_and_Equipment_Breakdown">
            <trigger>User asks about Service Line or Equipment Breakdown coverage.</trigger>
            <action>
                - Service Line: Pays to repair or replace underground utility lines (water, sewer, electrical, gas, internet) between street and home (homeowner's responsibility).
                - Equipment Breakdown: Covers sudden mechanical or electrical failure of home systems and appliances (HVAC, water heater, refrigerator, washer/dryer).
            </action>
        </step>

        <step name="Earthquake_Ordinance_and_Jewelry">
            <trigger>User asks about Earthquake, Ordinance of Law, or Scheduled Personal Property (Jewelry).</trigger>
            <action>
                - Earthquake Coverage: Covers structural and property damage from earthquakes with a 5% or 10% deductible.
                - Ordinance of Law: Pays additional costs to bring home up to current local building codes during rebuild/repair.
                - Scheduled Personal Property: Insures high-value items (jewelry, watches, art) beyond standard policy sub-limits for appraised value ($1,000 increments up to $10,000).
            </action>
        </step>

        <step name="Endorsements_and_Incident_Types">
            <trigger>User asks about Standard vs Extended Incidents, Mold, Mine Subsidence, Animal, Pool, or Personal Injury (Libel).</trigger>
            <action>
                - Standard vs Extended Incidents: Standard covers named perils (fire, theft, vandalism); Extended provides open-perils protection against all causes except explicit exclusions.
                - Mold Property Protection: Covers mold remediation to structure in available states (FL, GA, LA, MA, MS, NJ; $10k or $25k limits).
                - Mine Subsidence: Protects against collapse of underground mines.
                - Animal / Pool Liability: Extends liability protection for pet incidents or swimming pool injuries ($25k limit in CA/FL).
                - Personal Injury (Libel): Covers claims of libel, slander, defamation, or invasion of privacy.
            </action>
        </step>
    </subtask>

    <subtask name="Premium_and_Quote_Management">
        <step name="Premium_Discounts_and_Navigation">
            <trigger>User asks why home premium changed, discounts available, bundling savings, rebuild overruns, effective dates, or next steps.</trigger>

                - Discounts: Multi-product bundling (auto + home), partner affinity, and protective device discounts are applied automatically.
                - Rebuild Exceeding Limit: Without Extended Replacement Cost, you are responsible for costs exceeding Coverage A limit.
                - Effective Date: You can adjust policy start date during the quote flow.
                - Underwriter: APEX Home is underwritten by Homesite Insurance, an American Family Insurance company.
            </action>
        </step>
    </subtask>
